We recently had a short film made about Valiant Vineyards, and we are proud to share it with you.

In under two minutes it takes you through the places our guests know best and a few they usually don't see: the tasting room, the barrel cellar, the still, and the view from above the Missouri River at dusk. It also shows the hands behind the wines and spirits, from grapes and grain to the bottling line.

The winery has been in the Nygaard family since 1996, and it is still a family operation today. The film says it better than we can.
